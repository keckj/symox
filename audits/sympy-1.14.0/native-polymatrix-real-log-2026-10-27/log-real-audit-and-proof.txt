Native log_to_real audit and bounded port, 2026-10-27

AUDIT AND SCOPE
The established public entry is symox.integrals.rationaltools.ratint (there is no new symox.ratint export). Its real=True/default-real path passes each LRT pair to log_to_real. Its real=False path continues to RootSum. The old Python log_to_real constructs two temporary Dummies; substitutes t=u+i*v in h and q; expands and collects real/imaginary parts; computes a resultant eliminating v; calls roots(filter=R) and count_roots to establish completeness; enumerates u and v roots; pairs +/-v, using D.evalf(chop=True) as a candidate check; constructs Poly A/B/norm and calls log_to_atan; finally enumerates the real roots of q and adds r*log(h(r,x)). There was no orchestrating native kernel.
Existing Rust primitives: MPoly<Rat> and exact domain metadata; FractionPoly RF storage of QQ[t]/ZZ[t]; exact rational roots and resultants; quadratic NumberField/AN with scoped field arithmetic; dense Field Euclid. The prior log_to_atan recurrence was Rat-only; its identical internal recurrence is now generic over Field, leaving its public QQ wrapper unchanged.

Only exact stock Poly inputs are admitted: q univariate over QQ/ZZ in a distinct canonical commutative Symbol/Dummy t, squarefree degree 1 or 2; h univariate in canonical Symbol/Dummy x with rational coefficients polynomial in t. QQ[t]/ZZ[t] uses stored ring coefficients, not a legacy rational-function approximation. Other formal variables, multivariate h, fraction coefficient domains QQ(t), EX, algebraic/inexact/custom domains, custom types and protocols retain the entire old implementation. Bounds h degrees <=32 in each variable, <=1024 terms, coefficient numerator/denominator <=4096 bits select the route only. h=0, repeated q, and zero real/imaginary polynomials in a nonreal conjugate retain their old degeneracy/error paths. A refusal returns no partial result; the adapter then executes the complete old function.

EXACT ROOT AND BRANCH PROOF
Write q=A*t^2+B*t+C with rational A!=0, Delta=B^2-4AC!=0. Its real/imaginary parts at t=u+i*v are c=A*(u^2-v^2)+B*u+C and d=v*(2*A*u+B). Exactly, Res_v(c,d)=q(u)*(2*A*u+B)^2. This identity explains every candidate of the old algorithm, including its repeated centre root; it is not a numerical sampling argument.
Let u0=-B/(2A). If Delta<0, q has no real roots and the only real u candidate is u0. There c=A*(-Delta/(4A^2)-v^2); the two real v roots are +/-sqrt(-Delta/(4A^2)). The old pairing retains the positive root v0. Exactly d(u0,v0)=0 because 2*A*u0+B=0. The new kernel therefore replaces the numerical D.evalf filter with this exact algebraic identity. If Delta>0, q has precisely the two real roots u0+/-sqrt(Delta/(4A^2)). The additional centre candidate has no real v roots. At either actual real root c=-A*v^2, so v=0 is discarded by the old pairing. Only the same two final r*log(h(r,x)) terms remain. The leading sign of A changes their ordering, not their set or any logarithm argument. For q=A*t+B the unique real root is -B/A; the old auxiliary v=0 is discarded and the same real term remains.
For a nonreal pair let z=u0+i*v0. The polynomial recurrence (R,I)->(u0*R-v0*I,v0*R+u0*I) proves by induction, for every monomial, that h(z,x)=P(x)+i*Q(x). P,Q lie in the exact real field QQ(v0), and v0 is certified positive by the exact rational radicand. The rational-square case uses Rat; otherwise AN uses the irreducible pure quadratic modulus T^2-v0^2, with no precision loss or machine sign test. The norm is computed as the same polynomial P^2+Q^2. No unit, sign or logarithm argument is removed or rescaled.
The output is exactly the old representative u0*log(P^2+Q^2)+v0*log_to_atan(P,Q), and exactly the old r*log(h(r,x)) representatives for real roots. Real-root h substitution uses the ordinary native substitution operation, retaining each logarithm argument. The generic Euclidean recurrence uses the same degree swap (f,g)->(-g,f), unique field quotients/remainders, half-gcdex initial data, monic gcd, t=(h-s*a)/b, and argument (f*s+g*t)/h, in the same order. Hence the actual Bezout representatives, and the separate principal atan terms, agree; no atan addition/reciprocal identity or logarithm combination is applied. This proves the branch constants by the same recurrence, not merely by equal derivatives. The finite/complex substitutions and pole witnesses in tests supplement this identity proof.
